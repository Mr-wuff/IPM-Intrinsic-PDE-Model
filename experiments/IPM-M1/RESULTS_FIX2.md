### Mechanism evidence
Compared with PointwiseJetMLP, PointwiseOrderIPM:
- improves mean field error by ~11.7%;
- improves mean jet error by ~28.1%;
- improves mean gradient error by ~18.7%;
- wins the field-error comparison in 4/5 formal seeds.

Compared with the convolutional HolonomicIPM, the pointwise ordered model:
- has similar field error;
- reduces jet error by ~28%;
- reduces gradient error by ~16%;
- runs faster.

## Important negative result: Sobolev/spectral auxiliary loss is not helping
The NoSobolev ablation is slightly better than the full Sobolev-trained model on field, jet, gradient, and generator metrics. Therefore Sobolev/spectral loss should not be part of the defining IPM architecture.

## Field baseline comparison
FieldStateConv remains best in mean field error:
- FieldStateConv: 0.00893
- PointwiseOrderIPM: 0.01004

The gap is now only ~12.5%, while IPM has better mean jet and gradient fidelity.

IPM is within 1.25x of FieldStateConv on 4/5 PDE families. Advection is the remaining base-regime exception.

## Grid transfer
The pointwise IPM substantially improves field-level grid transfer:
- N=64: IPM 0.00841 vs FieldStateConv 0.09250
- N=128: IPM 0.00846 vs FieldStateConv 0.00838
- N=256: IPM 0.01179 vs FieldStateConv 0.05076
- N=512: IPM 0.03470 vs FieldStateConv 0.07384

This is strong evidence that removing index-space convolution improves discretization transfer.

However, high-order jet error at N=512 remains large, so derivative fidelity at extreme resolution is not solved.

## dt transfer
At 4x training dt:
- IPM field error: 0.06559
- FieldStateConv field error: 0.06514

IPM has a slightly better degradation ratio because its base error is higher, but absolute accuracy is essentially tied. Future gates should emphasize absolute and degradation metrics together.

## Parameter OOD
- IPM: 0.03410
- FieldStateConv: 0.03243

The gap is only ~5.1%.

## Noise robustness
IPM remains more sensitive to noisy input because derivatives amplify observation noise. This is now one of the clearest unresolved limitations of jet-state learning.

## First-principles interpretation
FIX2 validates three useful facts:
1. local differential coordinates are viable;
2. derivative-order structure is useful beyond simple concatenation;
3. pointwise jet-space generators generalize across grid resolutions better than index-space convolutions.

But FIX2 is **not the final IPM architecture**. It still represents the local law with generic MLP components and uses auxiliary training machinery.

The next architecture stage will therefore return to the mathematical object itself: a neural evolutionary vector field on jet space generated by a characteristic (Q_\theta), with Cartan total derivatives and intrinsic time evolution constructed from differential geometry rather than stacked neural modules.
