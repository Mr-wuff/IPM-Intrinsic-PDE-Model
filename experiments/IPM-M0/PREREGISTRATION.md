# IPM-M0 — Intrinsic Prolongation Architecture Qualification

## Scientific question
Can a neural PDE model make differential consistency an **architectural identity** by predicting only the base infinitesimal generator (Q_\theta=u_t) and generating higher derivative-state evolution through intrinsic prolongation, instead of predicting derivative evolution freely or enforcing consistency only through a loss?

## Null hypothesis
A capacity-matched derivative-feature or free-jet model performs as well as IPM on rollout, derivative-state fidelity, OOD transfer, and stability. Intrinsic prolongation adds no measurable benefit beyond derivative features or regularization.

## Proposed IPM mechanism
For a (k)-jet (J^k u), the learnable model predicts only:
[
Q_\theta(J^k u, \lambda) = u_t.
]
The remaining jet-state evolution is generated by:
[
\partial_t u_\alpha = D_\alpha Q_\theta.
]
The model is therefore not allowed to independently output ((u_x)_t,(u_{xx})_t,\ldots).

## PDE families
- Heat
- Linear advection
- Viscous Burgers
- Allen–Cahn
- KdV

All formal M0 systems are 1-D periodic scalar evolution PDEs. Wave equation is deferred because it requires a second-order temporal state formulation and should not be mixed into the first structural qualification.

## Primary causal comparison
SoftJetConsistency vs IPM-IntrinsicProlongation.

If the two models have comparable capacity and training budget, any consistent gain in long-horizon stability, jet fidelity, or transfer is evidence for the architectural constraint rather than merely adding derivative channels.

## M0 gates
M0 does NOT require IPM to dominate every baseline on every PDE.

Required:
- G0: numerical derivative validation passes.
- G1: intrinsic prolongation identity defect is at numerical tolerance.
- G2: IPM training is stable on all five PDEs.
- G3: IPM beats SoftJetConsistency on mean long-rollout jet error by >= 10% OR shows a >= 20% reduction in consistency defect with no >5% field-error regression.
- G4: dt-transfer degradation ratio is no worse than 1.25x SoftJetConsistency.
- G5: grid-transfer degradation ratio is no worse than 1.25x SoftJetConsistency.
- G6: coefficient-OOD mean rollout error is no worse than 1.10x the best non-IPM learned baseline.
- G7: no catastrophic instability (>10x error explosion or NaN) in the formal rollout horizon.
- G8: at least 4/5 seeds reproduce the direction of the main structural result.

Decision:
- PASS: G0,G1,G2,G7,G8 plus at least 3 of G3–G6.
- CONDITIONAL: structural identity passes but empirical advantage is mixed; redesign before A0.
- FAIL: intrinsic mechanism itself is unstable or gives no reproducible benefit.

## Paper rule
This experiment is a mechanism qualification only. Passing M0 permits IPM-A0. It is not sufficient for the paper's final performance claims.
